### 👨‍💻 Executive Summary

I am a **Senior Software Engineer** with over 5 years of production experience architecting and delivering high-stakes software solutions across **Healthcare**, **Government Digital Solutions**, **Enterprise ERPs**, and **Commercial Systems**.

Throughout my career, I have operated as the lead architect and primary developer delivering solutions from the ground up:
* 🏥 **National Healthcare Registries:** Lead Developer on nationwide clinical registries executed in multi-agency partnerships between the **Ministry of Health (MOH)**, **USAID**, and **Electronic Health Solutions (EHS / Hakeem)**.
* 🇸🇦 **Government Digital Transformation (Saudi Vision 2030):** Designed and deployed large-scale ERP platforms and governmental web applications in Saudi Arabia, including direct integration with Saudi Arabia's national digital identity verification platform (**Nafath**).
* 🔐 **Security & Infrastructure Hardening:** Deep expertise with **AES-256 encryption**, multi-tenant licensing engines, access control lists (ACLs), on-premises **Windows Server & IIS** configurations, and database query optimization.
* 🤖 **AI-Augmented Engineering:** Active practitioner of **AI agents** and AI-assisted development workflows to accelerate requirements modeling, system analysis, automated testing, and software delivery.
* 👥 **Mentorship & Technical Standards:** Proven track record conducting technical hiring interviews, establishing repository standards, and mentoring junior engineers, university graduates, and interns.

#### 1. National Healthcare Registries (EHS / MOH / USAID)
* **Jordan Cystic Fibrosis Registry (JCFR):** Acted as the **Lead Developer and primary point of contact** for a high-impact national registry developed in partnership between **USAID**, the **Ministry of Health (MOH)**, and **EHS**. Drove the architecture, database migrations, patch implementations, and final deployment at the Ministry of Health.
* **Jordan Health Registry (JHR):** Core lead developer for a central health registration platform collaborating with the Ministry of Health, engineered on **ASP.NET Core 9**, **Blazor**, **MariaDB/MySQL**, and **Radzen**.
* **Clinical Interoperability:** Architect of bidirectional transformation pipelines bridging legacy **HL7 v2** (`ER7` over `MLLP`) with modern **HL7 FHIR R4** (`RESTful JSON` via Firely SDK).

#### 2. Government Sector Solutions & Enterprise ERPs (Saudi Vision 2030)
* **Enterprise ERP Ecosystems:** Designed, engineered, and maintained comprehensive ERP platforms spanning **HR & vacations**, **procurement**, **marketing**, **warehousing**, **accounting**, and **project management** using `.NET Core`, `Angular`, `TypeScript`, `Blazor`, and `Microsoft SQL Server`.
* **National Digital Identity (Nafath Integration):** Implemented secure identity verification workflows connecting enterprise systems to Saudi Arabia's **Nafath platform** for citizen/resident national credential authentication.
* **Localization & Compliance:** Built enterprise-grade, localization-ready applications featuring seamless Arabic/English RTL support tailored for governmental operational standards.

#### 3. Reverse Engineering & Game Modding
* **Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord:** Advanced C# and .NET reflection modding utilizing Harmony bytecode hooking to modify campaign rules, battle AI, and asset systems.
* **Stardew Valley:** C# / SMAPI mods integrating custom event lifecycles, game-loop hooks, and save serialization.
* **Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition:** Engine data modding, trigger programming, and strategic AI balancing.
